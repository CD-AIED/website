I am a lecturer at the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, University of Bucharest, where I lead the AI in Education group. 
My work spans AI in education and Romanian NLP, with a focus on automated assessment, the construction of Romanian educational datasets, 
and the auditing of large language models used in learning contexts. I hold a PhD in combinatorics on words and serve as President of the Romanian 
National Informatics Olympiad and as a coach of the University of Bucharest’s ICPC teams.
